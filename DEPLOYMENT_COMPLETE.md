## 🚀 HOW TO GET YOUR APP LIVE (5 MINUTES)

Your app is built and ready in the `dist` folder. Follow these steps:

### Option 1: Deploy to Netlify (FREE - Recommended)

1. **Go to**: https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. **Drag** the `dist` folder onto the page
3. **Wait** 30 seconds - Your app is live!
4. **Add Environment Variables** in Netlify site settings:
   - VITE_SUPABASE_URL (from your .env file)
   - VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY (from your .env file)
5. **Redeploy** after adding env variables

**Your app will have a URL like**: `https://your-app-name.netlify.app`

### Option 2: Deploy to Vercel (FREE - Alternative)

1. Install Vercel CLI: `npm i -g vercel`
2. Run: `vercel --prod`
3. Follow prompts
4. Add environment variables in Vercel dashboard
